# UX Interaction Patterns: CHAMBER OVERDRIVE (膛室超载)

> **Status**: Approved
> **Last Updated**: 2026-11-08

## 1. Zero-Pause In-Run Interactions
- **Proximity `[E]` & Bet `[F]` Dual-Action Prompts**: Unlocking doors or approaching arena interactables displays inline contextual badges (`[E] 进门 | [F] 押注 25⚡`) without opening modal popups.
- **Inline `[7]/[8]/[9]/[G]` Swap-and-Salvage Strip**: When Passive Relic slots are full (`3/3`), standing near a 4th Relic displays a bottom comparison strip where one keypress swaps a slot or salvages the item into `🪙 Bounty Chips`.

## 2. Crosshair-Proximate `64px` Micro-HUD
- Dynamic combat states (weapon-specific Active Reload slider, Final Round pip, `RMB` Heavy ready bracket, and Graze Ring) appear within `64px` of the player/crosshair only when active and fade within `150ms`.
